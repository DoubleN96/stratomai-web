The funnel · How it works

Five steps, one ladder

Every number in this report is a measurement of how well people move through these five steps, not five separate metrics.

01
Someone sees Tudor's content, mostly organic, sometimes a paid ad, and lands on tudormorari.ai/lives.
02
They fill the form: name, email, WhatsApp. That's the lead.
03
They get the free prompt packs and can register for the free Sunday live class.
04
They join the WhatsApp community, where every future pack, replay, and the eventual price announcement land.
05
On 9 August, the paid community opens, and the same people are invited to convert into paying members at the founding price.

This is the ladder the whole report below is built around: organic or paid traffic feeds a free funnel of prompts and live classes, the free funnel feeds a WhatsApp community, and that community is where the sale actually happens once the doors open on 9 August.

What still leaks

Walking the funnel end to end as a stranger would surfaces four real leaks, ranked by cost rather than by how easy they are to fix.

WhatsApp isn't a broadcast yet
Mass sending is blocked by Meta (test number, unverified business). We can announce packs one by one, we cannot run a closing campaign to 1,761 people on launch day.
The live is a blind spot
The hottest moment of the whole funnel runs on YouTube, off our domain. We do not know who watched, how long, or who left right before the offer.
No rung between free and $49.99
The whole journey is free, free, free, then a recurring charge with nothing in between. Worth deciding before 9 August whether that gap needs a step.
The database has noise
22,500 of 27,595 GHL contacts are Instagram handles with no email, from the IG autoreply. They dilute every metric and every Meta audience built from the list.

Not a leak: the community itself. 1,761 members, 35% conversion from the form, that step works, the instinct to fix it would be the wrong instinct.

At this stage of the funnel, broad cheap reach is outperforming precision targeting. Unusual, but worth taking seriously rather than assuming the lookalike just needs more time.

One creative carries it
The Rasputin angle, the same historical hook that already went viral organically, produced 66 of 108 leads (61%) at the second-best cost per lead in the account. This account's performance right now is the performance of one proven creative, not the campaign structure.
The open question
We lose 82% of clicks between ad and landing page (1,076 link clicks, 189 page views). Either real drop-off inside Instagram's in-app browser, or a measurement gap. Until tested, we don't know if the real CPL is 5.90 EUR or meaningfully better. Highest-leverage thing to figure out before spending more.

Recommendation, in order

Fix or diagnose the click-to-page leak before raising budget at all, spending into a leaking funnel just wastes more money at the same leak point.

Pause the buyer lookalike, it isn't earning its cost at 3-4× the price of cold broad.

Make more Rasputin variations rather than untested new angles, it's the one creative already proven to convert.

Hold off scaling spend until the week of 2-9 August, the first week there's actually something to sell.
