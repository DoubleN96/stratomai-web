03What still leaks

Ranked by cost, not by ease.

Leak 1 · high

WhatsApp cannot be a broadcast channel yet

Mass sending is blocked by Meta, test number, unverified business. We can announce packs one by one, we cannot run a closing campaign to 1,761 people on launch day.

Leak 2 · high

The live happens where we cannot see it

The hottest moment of the whole funnel runs on YouTube, off our domain. We do not know who watched, how long, or who left right before the offer.

Leak 3 · medium

No step between free and $49.99

The whole journey is free, free, free, then a recurring charge with nothing in between. Worth deciding before 9 August whether that gap needs a rung.

Leak 4 · medium

Paid ads are not the engine

81 leads against 2,204 organic. Either the budget split is wrong, the attribution is wrong, or both. Worth knowing which before spending more.

Leak 5 · medium

The database has noise in it

22,500 of 27,595 GHL contacts are Instagram handles with no email, from the autoreply. They dilute every metric and every Meta audience built from the list.

Not a leak

The community itself

1,761 members, 35% conversion from the form. This step works. The instinct to fix it was the wrong instinct, corrected in section 01.

04Going into 9 August

Point the budget at what actually leaks.

The funnel is not broken. The two things worth real attention before launch are the ones with no easy screen to point at: what happens inside YouTube during the live, and whether WhatsApp can carry a closing push once the business is verified with Meta.

  • Verify the WhatsApp business account with Meta. Without it, the 9 August close cannot use the channel where 1,761 warm people already are.
  • Instrument the live somehow. Even a simple poll or a link click at the moment of the offer tells us more than we know today.
  • Decide on a mid step, or decide against one, on purpose. Not deciding is itself a decision.
The organic reel outperforms the ad budget by 27 times. Fix the plumbing before adding more water.
