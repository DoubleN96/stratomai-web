07One directionNo leaks

Every asset points at one next action.

A lead should never have to choose. Today the journey has several exits of equal weight, and each one is a place to lose somebody. Before the launch there is exactly one destination: the free WhatsApp group. After the launch there is exactly one: the community. Everything else is a delivery mechanism, not a destination.

  • The leak: two forms. /lives captures the lead, /live captures the registration. Merge them.
  • The leak: the tutorial on YouTube. It sends people out with nothing bringing them back. It ends with the same single CTA, in the description and spoken.
  • The leak: the Drive download. Getting the file currently ends the journey. The file itself carries the next step on its last page.
  • The leak: email and WhatsApp competing. They are one message in two places, not two channels with two jobs. Same words, same single CTA.
One CTA per asset. Never two buttons of equal weight.

09The structure

Give one thing fully. Name what you did not give.

The same five beats for every video and every lead magnet, so nobody has to reinvent the format each week, and so every free thing ends in the same place.

1. Hook, three seconds. The result, or the mistake everyone makes.
2. Proof. Show the finished shot before explaining anything.
3. Teach one thing, completely. The smallest piece that works on its own. Not a teaser, a real usable win.
4. Open the loop. Name what you did not cover. "This is one of the three settings that matter. The other two are what stop the face flickering."
5. The same CTA, every time. "The full workflow is inside the community."
The rule for every lead magnet It delivers ONE working piece, never the system. It has to produce a visible result in under ten minutes, and it has to make obvious that there is more. If someone can finish it and feel done, it is the wrong magnet.
